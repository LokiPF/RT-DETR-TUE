% Our reference builds on a division of labor inside deep networks. Style transfer represents an image's content by the responses of a deep layer and its style by feature-map statistics~\cite{gatys2016style}. Channel means and variances alone suffice to transfer a style~\cite{huang2017adain}. Appearance variation such as color and style is encoded mainly in shallow layers, content mainly in deep ones~\cite{pan2018ibnnet}. Consistent with this, mixing the channel statistics of shallow layers between images simulates new domains~\cite{zhou2021mixstyle}, and adapting to image corruptions works best when only the first block is fine-tuned~\cite{lee2023surgical}. Corruptions shift these statistics: merely re-estimating the batch-normalization statistics on corrupted images raises the ImageNet-C accuracy of a ResNet-50 from 39.2\% to 50.7\%~\cite{schneider2020improving}. Full-spectrum OOD detection uses the split in the opposite direction. Its SEM score divides a density of first-layer channel statistics out of a deep-feature density, treating the two as independent, so that covariate shift is tolerated and only unseen classes are flagged~\cite{yang2023fsood}.
%
% To detect covariate shift, we instead judge the shallow statistics given the content. This is the principle of conditional anomaly detection, which judges a sample's indicator attributes given its environmental ones~\cite{song2007conditional}, for instance against normal samples with similar environmental attributes~\cite{valko2008distance}. CovariateFlow~\cite{viviers2024generative} follows a similar idea in pixel space, with a flow that models fine image detail given coarse content. Closest in structure, SPADE~\cite{cohen2020spade} retrieves the 50 nearest normal images by the deepest pooled feature of an ImageNet network, then densely matches the features of the network's first three blocks against them to localize defects. To our knowledge, no monitor uses the nearest clean scenes, retrieved in a detector's own deepest stage, as a per-image reference for the channel statistics of its earlier stages, to detect corruption without any training.
